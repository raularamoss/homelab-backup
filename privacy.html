Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026.

Purpose

HomeLab Backup is a private, non-commercial application used exclusively by its owner to store encrypted HomeLab backups in the owner's Google Drive account.

Google Drive access

The application requests access only to files and folders it creates. It does not access other files already stored in Google Drive.

Data use and sharing

Data is used only to create, verify, retain, and restore encrypted backups. No information is sold, shared, transferred, or used for advertising or analytics.

Storage and deletion

Backup content is encrypted before upload. The owner controls retention and deletion. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings.
